The first release signed with Fretlight's own release key. The app listens better in real rooms, plays you each song's guitar part, and has a more accurate tuner and a more natural guitar sound.

> **Uninstall 0.7.0 first.** Earlier builds were signed with a debug key, and Android will not update an app to a different signature. Uninstalling removes your saved songs, custom tunings and settings from 0.7.0. Later releases will install over this one and keep your data.
>
> **Pre-release.** The app ID is still a placeholder. Accuracy with a real guitar and phone is not yet measured.

## New in 0.8.0

### Hear the song and play along
- **Hear the guitar part.** Press play and the app strums each song's chords at its tempo, so you can compare it with your own playing. The guitar sound is generated on the phone; there is no melody, because ChordPro sheets don't include one.
- **Position and tempo sliders** in the player: jump to any bar, set the tempo from 40 to 200 BPM.
- **Strum patterns** (one per bar, every beat, folk) and an optional click, in the song's Arrange sheet.

### Listening
- **Adapts to your room.** Listening screens learn the background noise level, so quiet playing is still heard and fans or hum are ignored.
- **Mic level meter** on the Tune, Identify and Practice screens shows whether the app hears you.
- **Sensitivity setting** (Settings → Listening: Low, Normal, High) for louder or quieter rooms.
- **Chord detection** holds up better with background noise, tells maj7 chords from plain ones, and counts one strum as one strum.

### Tuner and sound
- **More accurate tuner.** It measures the string's fundamental, so real steel strings no longer read 2–4 cents sharp. Readings stay within about a cent in a noisy room (previously up to 9 cents).
- **More natural guitar sound** for chord playback and songs: warmer, with the upper harmonics fading first, as on a real string. Pitch was already exact; the old sound was just too bright.
- Readings that round to zero now show "0¢" instead of "−0¢".

### Your own chords and songs
- **Draw your own chords** on a fretboard grid (Chords → +). The app names a shape from its notes, and saved shapes appear under Your chords.
- **Practise a chord** from its page: drill it alone, or switch between it and another chord.
- **Song editor buttons** for title, verse, chorus and chord names.

### Songs
- Every starter song was **published before 1900**, and each sheet names its source. Four songs were replaced (Red River Valley, Down in the Valley, When the Saints, Shenandoah), because their familiar versions may still be under copyright. They were replaced with Aura Lee, Simple Gifts, Jingle Bells and Beautiful Dreamer. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

## Which file?

| File | For |

| `fretlight-0.8.0-arm64-v8a.apk` | Most current Android phones. Start here. |
| `fretlight-0.8.0-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older 32-bit phones |

| `fretlight-0.8.0-universal.apk` | Any of the above (larger download) |

Requires Android 7.0 or later. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. No iPhone build is attached.

## Known limits

- Chord identification and practice are tested on synthesised and recorded test signals; accuracy with a real guitar and phone is still unmeasured.
- The progression pack is licensed for non-commercial use only: remove it before any paid or ad-supported release.
