A clearer app, a place to practise chords, and fairer listening for guitars that sound different.

> **Pre-release.** Installs over 0.9.x and keeps your progress, songs, chords and settings; you won't see the welcome questions. Listening is measured on synthesised guitars through modelled mics; it has not been measured with a real guitar yet.

## New layout

- **Four labelled tabs:** Home, Practice, Songs and Tools. Every tab always shows its name and stays in the same place.
- **Tuner from anywhere.** A Tuner button sits at the top of every screen, including lessons and songs. Back returns you to where you were.
- **Home** shows your next lesson, today's goal and workout, quick buttons for Metronome, Identify and Settings, and the songs you played last.
- **Advanced options are tucked away, not gone:** reference pitch, custom tunings and the frequency readout on the tuner; subdivisions on the metronome; other shapes and notes on a chord's page.
- **Clearer buttons:** song actions (Practise, Key and capo, Edit) and the chord library's Favourites and Draw your own now have labels, not just icons.
- **Leaving a lesson halfway** asks first, including the back gesture.
- **A two-question welcome** for new installs (how much you've played, which hand you strum with). It's skippable. New players go straight into a five-minute first session: tune up, E minor, then a change to A minor.

## Practise chords

- **Drill a chord:** tap any chord on the Practice tab for five clean strums. A ring shows how well you know each one.
- **Chords:** every chord the course teaches, by stage, none locked.
- **Changes:** pick any two chords and switch between them (it waits for you), or race the clock for a minute. Your best for each pair is kept.
- **Practice now counts.** Drills, races, progressions and song practice add to your daily minutes, streak, XP and chord mastery. Drills don't add stars, so stars still show how far through the course you are.

## Chord matching

Guitars, strings and mics colour a chord differently: a quiet G string makes Em sound like E5, and a barre with one dead string loses a note. **Settings → Listening → Chord matching** chooses how closely a strum must match:

- **Exact:** only the exact chord counts.
- **Normal** (default): the chord, or the same chord with a note missing or added (E5 for Em).
- **Forgiving:** also same-root chords with a different colour note (sus, add), and readings a little further down the list. For unusual-sounding guitars, old strings, or barre chords that don't ring fully.

**Fixed:** the wrong chord could count. With D asked for, a Dm strum passed, because its notes also look like D5. The app now checks the notes it hears: if the opposite third rings clearly, or too much of the sound is outside the chord, the strum doesn't count. This applies on every setting.

## Smoother

- The lesson list opens without a hitch: lessons are built as they scroll into view.
- Fixed an error when closing a lesson halfway, and when leaving Identify, practice or a lesson while the microphone was still starting.

## How it was checked

- **Chord matching:** every chord the course teaches, plus ten common barre shapes (A, G, C, D, Am, Gm, Cm, F♯m, B♭ and B), on five guitar tones: steel, bright new strings, nylon, old dull strings, and a thin bodiless guitar. Each was played five ways: normal, slow, through a headset mic, unevenly, and from a distance in noise. A third of the strums also had a weak or muted third or an out-of-tune guitar. Played cleanly, Normal counts 799 of 800 and Forgiving 799 of 800. With a weak or muted third, Normal counts 85 of 96 and Forgiving 93 of 96. With the wrong chord played (26 common mix-ups), when its third rang clearly Normal let through 1 in 104 (10 before this fix) and Forgiving 5 in 104.
- **Smoothness:** frame times were measured on an iPhone 17 simulator (debug build) while switching tabs, flinging every list, playing along, running the metronome and listening with the tuner. Scrolling and playback hold 60 fps; the few slow frames are when a screen first opens.
- **Tests:** 475 automated tests, including tap counts to every feature, the tuner from every screen, Back behaviour, and an upgrade from a 0.9 database keeping all data.
