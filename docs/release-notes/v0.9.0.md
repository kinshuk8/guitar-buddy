Fretlight now teaches. A new **Learn** tab takes you from your first chord to barre chords up the neck, with short lessons that listen to what you play, and stars, levels and a daily streak to keep you going.

> **Pre-release.** Installs over 0.8.0 and keeps your songs, chords and settings (same signing key). If you still have 0.7.0 or earlier, uninstall it first. The lessons are tested on synthesised and recorded test audio; they have not yet been tried with a real guitar.

## New in 0.9.0

### Learn: 55 lessons in four stages
- **Beginner:** string names, tuning up, E minor and A minor, your first chord change, down-strums on the beat, D and G, and Clementine as a first song.
- **Improver:** A, E, C and D minor; C to G; the folk strum and three-four time; notes on the low strings; reading tab; three songs.
- **Adept:** an easy F, then the full F barre; sevenths, sus chords and B minor; faster changes and the four-chord loop; Ode to Joy and Twinkle from tab; three more songs.
- **Mastery:** barre shapes up the neck; I–IV–V in G, D, A, E and C; minor keys; notes across the neck; changes at 40+ a minute.

Lessons open in order. If you already play, start at any later stage with **I can already play this: start here**.

### Games that listen
- **Chord drills and changes** wait until they hear the right chord.
- **One-minute change races** count clean changes and keep your best for each pair. The clock starts with your first chord.
- **Strum in time:** a one-bar count-in, then every strum is timed against the beat. Your phone's audio delay is taken out first, so you're scored on how steady you are.
- **Notes, tab and tuning:** play the note shown, follow a line of tab note by note, or tune each string to within 10 cents.
- **Quizzes, no guitar needed:** name the string, the note at a fret, the chord in a diagram, or the note a tab number plays. Right answers in a row build a combo.

### Progress
- Up to three stars per lesson; XP and levels; six skill bars (notes, chords, changes, rhythm, reading, songs).
- A daily practice goal (5 to 30 minutes) and a streak.
- A mastery ring for every chord you have met.
- **Daily chord workout:** drills your two weakest chords, races you between them, then runs through your four weakest.
- Settings → Learning → **Reset learning progress** clears it all (your songs, chords and settings stay).

### Also
- Learn is the first tab and where the app opens; the tuner is one tap away.

## Known limits

- Lessons that listen are tuned on synthesised strums and notes; how they behave with a real guitar and phone mic is still unmeasured.
- In a noisy room, a soft up-strum right after a loud down-strum can be missed by the strum-in-time game.
- The progression pack is licensed for non-commercial use only: remove it before any paid or ad-supported release.
