The microphone no longer stops for notifications, and never stays paused.

> **Pre-release.** Installs over 0.10.x and keeps your progress, songs, chords and settings.

## Fixed

- **A notification stopped the microphone until you restarted the app (Android).** A notification sound briefly takes Android's audio focus, which only governs playback; recording carries on fine. The app took it for a phone call, stopped listening and let go of its audio session, so Android never told it the notification had finished. Audio focus no longer stops the mic on Android; during a phone call, Android silences the input itself.
- **A paused screen stayed paused.** The tuner, lessons, practice and Identify all saw an interruption even when they weren't open, and kept waiting for an end the system doesn't always send. Now an interruption only pauses the screen that was listening, and opening that screen again or coming back to the app starts listening again.
- **Lessons and practice went quiet without saying why.** Every listening screen now shows "Paused while another app uses the microphone" with a **Resume** button.

On iOS, a phone call or Siri still pauses listening, and it resumes when they finish.
