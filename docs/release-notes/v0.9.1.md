Chords are named right more often, especially with a mic close to the guitar.

> **Pre-release.** Installs over 0.9.0 and 0.8.0 and keeps your progress, songs, chords and settings. Accuracy is measured on synthesised guitars through modelled mics; it has not been measured with a real guitar yet.

## Fixed

- **Em no longer reads as E5, and Am as A5.** A down-strum reaches the third (Em's open G, Am's C) after the root and fifth, and the app used to lock onto the power chord it heard first. It now switches to the full chord once the third comes through.
- **With a mic close to the guitar** (wired earphones held by the soundhole, say), the bass strings sound much louder than the treble ones, and chords lost their colour note: D read D5, A read A5, G7 read G, and Bm, Bb, Fmaj7, Dsus2 and Asus2 went the same way. String loudness now matters much less.
- **Asus4 no longer reads D5, and Esus4 no longer reads A5.**
- **The B minor lesson** now shows the shape it describes (x24432, the A minor shape moved up), not the barre with F# in the bass.
- **The Identify screen** waits for a strum to settle before naming it, so wrong names no longer flash by while the strings are still being hit.

## How it was checked

Every chord the lessons teach, plus common extras, under ten conditions: fast, slow and up-strums; a close mic hearing the bass strings 9 dB and 18 dB louder; uneven picking; and a phone further away, slightly out of tune, in room noise. Before: 38 of 296 chord/condition pairs named wrong. After: every chord the lessons teach is right under realistic conditions. The only misses are Gmaj7 (not taught) and, under the extreme 18 dB mic, G7 and Dsus2, which read as G and D5 (lessons accept these as close enough). Full table: `docs/accuracy-report-m5.md`.
