Chordonomicon progressions pack

Source: Chordonomicon, https://huggingface.co/datasets/ailsntua/Chordonomicon
Authors: Spyridon Kantarelis, Konstantinos Thomas, Vassilis Lyberatos,
Edmund Dervakos, Giorgos Stamou.
Paper: CHORDONOMICON: A Dataset of 666,000 Songs and their Chord
Progressions. arXiv:2410.22046, 2024.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Changes made: the dataset was reduced to aggregate statistics of repeating
3-4 chord loops (how many songs use each loop, their most common genres and
decades). No per-song data, song or artist identifiers, or dates are
included. Built by tool/chordonomicon/build_pack.dart.

This pack is optional and can be removed from the app without affecting
anything else.
